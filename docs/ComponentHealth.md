# ComponentHealth

A component's health as ccluster reports it: Argo CD's health statuses, plus Succeeded and Failed for a component that runs to completion, such as a job.

## Enum

* `HEALTHY` (value: `'Healthy'`)

* `PROGRESSING` (value: `'Progressing'`)

* `DEGRADED` (value: `'Degraded'`)

* `SUSPENDED` (value: `'Suspended'`)

* `MISSING` (value: `'Missing'`)

* `UNKNOWN` (value: `'Unknown'`)

* `SUCCEEDED` (value: `'Succeeded'`)

* `FAILED` (value: `'Failed'`)
